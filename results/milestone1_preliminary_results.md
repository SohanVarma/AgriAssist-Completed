# Milestone 1 Preliminary Results

## Data Pipeline
The data pipeline created 120 sample crop disease images, validated metadata, and created train/validation splits.

## Initial Model
A small CNN was trained for 5 epochs. Preliminary validation accuracy reached 1.000 on the sample validation split.

## RAG + Localization Demo
A lightweight retrieval demo generated India-specific advice with confidence and safety notes.

## Synthetic Data Demo
A synthetic rare-disease augmentation demo created 10 additional leaf_spot images. The final project will replace this with a diffusion-based generator.
